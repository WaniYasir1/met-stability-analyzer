Data Loss Warnings

🔴
Multi-allelic Truncation
Loci with >4 alleles lose rare-allele info. Alleles ranked 5+ encoded as N.
🟡
Stepwise Signal Lost
Allele size relationships (SSR evolution signal) are not representable in ACGT codes.
🟡
Dominant Heterozygotes
0/1 data cannot distinguish AA from AB — heterozygosity is lost.
💡
Best Practice
Frequency-rank encoding + transparent methods reporting is the most defensible approach.

Auto-Generated Methods

SSR marker data were converted to HapMap format for GWAS analysis using GenoConvert (v0.1). Co-dominant SSR alleles (base-pair sizes) were encoded as nucleotide symbols using frequency-rank encoding, where the most frequent allele was assigned A, and subsequent alleles C, G, and T respectively. Loci with >4 alleles had rare alleles encoded as N (missing). Dominant (0/1) markers were encoded as A (present) and T (absent). Loci with MAF < 0.05 or >20% missing data were excluded prior to analysis.

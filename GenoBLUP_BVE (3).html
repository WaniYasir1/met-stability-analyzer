Selection Index Setup Hazel (1943) / Smith (1936)
ℹ
The selection index I = b'x maximises correlation with the breeding goal H = v'a. Index weights: b = P⁻¹Gv where P = phenotypic var-cov, G = genetic cov between index traits and goal traits, v = economic weights.
